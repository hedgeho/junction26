Privacy

Agora stores your Bluesky identity, feedback, configuration history, note snapshots and selected post references and references to comments and quote posts made through Agora on the application host.

The public landing page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's request.

Public posts, the images attached to them, and explicit public preferences go to Gemini. Private notes, private feedback and inferred interests are processed by Gemma on Modal. This is cloud processing.

You can connect Google Drive folders containing Markdown notes. For link-shared folders, Context.dev processes the shared folder pages and listings to access their contents; Agora downloads original Markdown directly from Google. Anyone with the link can access shared notes. Read the Context.dev privacy policy. Private folders may be shared with the app's service account as a Viewer. Note contents are analyzed by Gemma on Modal. Source files and existing posts are never edited. You can choose to delete your own comments on Bluesky. When you choose to interact, Agora sends your like, repost, bookmark, comment or quote post to Bluesky on your behalf. Avatars and post images load directly from the platform's media servers. If Threads is enabled, your public search terms are sent to Meta's Threads API; private notes are not.

Disconnecting notes removes that folder's imported snapshots and matches. Configuration history may retain previously inferred interests. Provider retention, backups and account erasure are managed by the host operator.
