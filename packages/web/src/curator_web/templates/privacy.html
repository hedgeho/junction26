Privacy

Agora stores your Bluesky identity, feedback, configuration history, note snapshots and selected post references on the application host.

Public posts, the images attached to them, and explicit public preferences go to Gemini. Private notes, private feedback and inferred interests are processed by Gemma on Modal. This is cloud processing.

Notes come from a local Markdown folder or a private Google Drive folder shared with the app's service account as a Viewer. Drive downloads use read-only API credentials. Source files and existing posts are never edited. When you choose to interact, Agora sends your like, repost, bookmark, comment or quote post to Bluesky on your behalf. Avatars and post images load directly from the platform's media servers.

Disconnecting notes removes that folder's imported snapshots and matches. Configuration history may retain previously inferred interests. Provider retention, backups and account erasure are managed by the host operator.
