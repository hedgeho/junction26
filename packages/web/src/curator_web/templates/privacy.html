Privacy

This feed stores public Bluesky posts for up to 48 hours to rank them, and honours deletions. For people who sign in, it stores their DID, handle, the feed description and feedback they write, and the feed interactions the Bluesky app sends (such as "show more/less"). Post text and the reader's description are sent to Anthropic's API to score relevance.
