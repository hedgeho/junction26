How your feed works

01

Find candidates

Agora gathers recent posts from people you follow and posts they liked or reposted, your Bluesky For You feed, matches for terms from your public preference profile, and public posts gaining attention quickly. Private notes are not used for discovery.

02

Screen and assess

Rules screen for patterns such as repeated campaigns, excessive posting and moderation labels. Gemini assesses relevance, substance and opportunity against your public profile. When enabled, separate Gemini Decisions checks flag content such as spam and ragebait.

03

Rank and select

Eligible posts are ranked using relevance (65%), substance (25%) and opportunity (10%), adjusted for age and content flags. Selection limits repeated authors, conversations, text and linked stories; it can reserve space for followed authors and nearby interests.

04

Learn from you

Feedback and its associated post go to Gemma to update a Markdown preference profile and search terms. If you start without a profile, Gemini drafts one from your own recent public posts. Your public profile is used in future Gemini assessments. The original post is not changed, and your feedback is not posted.

05

Match your notes

If notes are connected, Gemma checks potentially relevant note passages against selected posts after the feed is built. Matching uses your private profile; your notes are not sent to Gemini, and the original files stay unchanged.

A preliminary selection can appear while filtering runs. Settings and model availability can change which stages run.
