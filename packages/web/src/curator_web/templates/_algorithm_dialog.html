    How our ranking compares with Opus

      01

      Find candidates

      Agora gathers recent posts from people you follow and posts they liked or reposted, your Bluesky For You feed, matches for terms from your public preference profile, and public posts gaining attention quickly. Private notes are not used for discovery.

      02

      Screen and assess

      Rules screen for patterns such as repeated campaigns, excessive posting and moderation labels. {% if gemma_scoring %}Gemma assesses relevance, substance and opportunity against your feed curation file.{% else %}Gemini assesses relevance, substance and opportunity against your feed curation file.{% endif %} When enabled, separate Gemini Decisions checks flag content such as spam and ragebait.

      03

      Rank and select

      Eligible posts are ranked using relevance (65%), substance (25%) and opportunity (10%), adjusted for age and content flags. Selection limits repeated authors, conversations, text and linked stories; it can reserve space for followed authors and nearby interests. When Gemma is available, it makes a final selection from the eligible candidates. The result never exceeds your post limit.

      04

      Learn from you

      Feedback and its associated post go to Gemma to update a Markdown preference profile and search terms. {% if not gemma_scoring %}If you start without a profile, Gemini may draft one from your own recent public posts.{% endif %} Tell your feed saves your preference for future assessments.{% if gemma_scoring %} Note-derived interests can also update this file and remain on the Gemma path.{% else %} Notes do not update this file while Gemini filtering is enabled.{% endif %} The original post is not changed, and your feedback is not posted.

      05

      Match your notes

      If notes are connected, Gemma checks potentially relevant note passages against selected posts after the feed is built. Matching uses your private profile; your notes are not sent to Gemini, and the original files stay unchanged.

      A preliminary selection can appear while filtering runs. Settings and model availability can change which stages run.
