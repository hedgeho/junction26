I · The square

Every Greek
city had one.

A square where people traded goods, news and ideas. Where you could hear a philosopher in the morning and argue politics by the afternoon. Where a city made up its mind, together.

Open to anyone. Every voice, every idea, every corner of the world, one tap away.

III · The sale

Feeds learned to reward outrage. The conversations we came for got harder to find.
