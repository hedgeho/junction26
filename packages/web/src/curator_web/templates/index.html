I · The square

Every Greek city had one.

A square where people traded goods, news and ideas. Where you could hear a philosopher in the morning and argue politics by the afternoon. Where a city made up its mind, together with intent.

II · The promise

The internet was supposed to be the biggest square ever built.

Open to anyone. Every voice, every idea, every corner of the world, one tap away.

III · The sale

Then someone noticed that anger keeps people scrolling.

Feeds learned to reward outrage. Coordinated networks learned to pass for public opinion. And the square was sold, one minute of our attention at a time.

IV · We stay

We're not leaving.

Our friends are here. The news is here. The ideas we want to engage with are here. The global conversations we want to contribute to are here.
