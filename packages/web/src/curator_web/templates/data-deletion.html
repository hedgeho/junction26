Data deletion

Agora has no accounts for Threads users and does not ask you to log in with Threads. Removing Agora from your Threads app permissions therefore needs nothing else from us.

What Agora keeps about Threads: public posts found by keyword search, with their text, public links, author username and media references. They are deleted automatically, usually within 48 hours of being collected. A post you delete on Threads is gone from Agora at that point at the latest.

To have a Threads post or username removed sooner{% if contact_email %}, email {{ contact_email }}{% else %}, contact the operator of this site{% endif %} with the post link or username. Requests are handled by hand.

Agora's Bluesky readers can see what is stored about them on the privacy page.
